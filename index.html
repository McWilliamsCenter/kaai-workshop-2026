Overview

About the workshop

This workshop is small and informal by design. It brings together a technically literate audience to discuss cutting-edge work at the intersection of foundation models, agentic methods, and astrophysical simulation — with the goal of identifying important directions in an emerging field and building new collaborations.

The event combines a conference — talks and discussion — with a hackathon, giving participants both a forum for ideas and a venue to build something with them.

This is the first workshop held under the Simons Foundation Keystone Astronomy & AI (KAAI) Fellows Program.

Topics

Scientific topics

Foundation models for astrophysics

How foundation models can be used to represent, manipulate, compress, compare, and interpret astrophysical simulations and observations.

Agentic science loops

How agentic methods can responsibly augment the scientific research process.
