Overview

About the workshop

Astrophysical simulations are expensive to generate, high-dimensional, and often difficult to compare or interpret directly — conditions where foundation models, which learn shared, transferable representations from large and heterogeneous datasets, are a natural fit for representing, compressing, and analyzing simulated and observed data alike. At the same time, agentic methods are beginning to reshape parts of the scientific process itself, from experiment design to analysis, raising open questions about how such tools can responsibly augment scientific research. This workshop brings together researchers working on both fronts to discuss recent progress and how these advances are likely to impact the field going forward.

Foundation models for astrophysics

How foundation models can be used to represent, manipulate, compress, compare, and interpret astrophysical simulations and observations.
